1. Project Title and Description
Project Title:
Shop wise AI cart 
Project Description:
Shop wise AI cart  is an AI-powered mobile app that helps users shop in a smarter, safer, and more 
budget-friendly way. The system uses the phone camera to scan items directly using AI image recognition. 
It identifies products, shows details like price and ingredients, and provides real-time alerts such as 
budget warnings, allergy notifications, and smart suggestions based on user behavior.

3. Background Research
Summarize existing solutions or research related to your project.Explain gaps your project will address.
Some existing solutions include image recognition apps, shopping assistants, and budget tracking tools. 
However, these systems are separate and do not provide real-time support during shopping.
