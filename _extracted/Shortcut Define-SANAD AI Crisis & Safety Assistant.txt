SANAD AI Crisis & Safety Assistant
SANAD AI Crisis & Safety Assistant is an AI-powered safety and crisis-awareness mobile application designed to help users understand potentially dangerous information and respond more safely during emergencies.
The application addresses a common problem during crises: the rapid spread of confusing, misleading, or false information. SANAD allows users to submit a news message, social media post, or alert and receive an AI-supported analysis of its potential safety level and credibility.
The application provides users with a simple and understandable result, including a safety level, possible threat type, affected area, and recommended actions. It also provides educational safety information and an Emergency Mode to help users quickly access important guidance during critical situations.
SANAD focuses on three main crisis categories: Conflict & War, Natural Disasters, and Health Crises, while also providing a General Mode for information that does not fit into a specific category.
The goal of SANAD is not to replace official emergency services or trusted authorities, but to help users become more aware, informed, and prepared when encountering potentially dangerous information.

Project Objectives
The main objectives of SANAD are to:
Improve crisis awareness by helping users understand information related to emergencies and potential threats.
Identify potentially misleading information by analyzing user-submitted news, messages, and alerts.
Provide clear safety guidance that users can easily understand and follow.
Reduce confusion during emergencies by organizing information according to different crisis categories.
Promote responsible information sharing by encouraging users to verify information before trusting or spreading it.
Support emergency preparedness through educational safety tips and an accessible Emergency Mode.
Use artificial intelligence for practical safety applications in a simple and user-friendly mobile application.

Key Features
AI-Powered Information Analysis
Users can paste a news message, social media post, or emergency-related information into SANAD. The system analyzes the content and provides an understandable assessment.
Safety Level
The application presents a simple safety classification:
Low — limited or low-risk information.
Medium — information that may require attention or verification.
High — information that may indicate a serious safety concern.
Information Credibility
SANAD categorizes submitted information as Real, Suspicious, or Fake based on its analysis, while encouraging users to verify important information through trusted official sources.
Crisis Categories
Users can select the type of situation they are dealing with:
Conflict & War — such as air raids, missiles, and armed threats.
Natural Disasters — such as floods, storms, and sandstorms.
Health Crisis — such as disease outbreaks, pandemics, and lockdowns.
General Mode — for other safety-related situations.
Threat and Area Identification
The application can identify the possible type of threat and relevant affected area from the submitted information when sufficient information is available.
Personalized Safety Recommendations
SANAD provides practical safety guidance based on the detected situation and selected crisis category.
Emergency Mode
Emergency Mode provides quick access to important safety guidance and emergency-related information when users need it most.
Trusted Sources
The application encourages users to verify critical information through reliable and official sources rather than relying only on social media or unverified messages.
User-Friendly Interface
SANAD is designed with a simple interface so that users can quickly understand the results without needing technical knowledge.


# How SANAD Works

SANAD follows a simple process designed to make crisis-related information easier to understand.
**Step 1 — Select a Crisis Category**
The user selects the relevant category, such as Conflict & War, Natural Disasters, Health Crisis, or General Mode.
**Step 2 — Submit Information**
The user pastes a news article, message, social media post, or other crisis-related information into the application.
**Step 3 — AI Analysis**
SANAD analyzes the submitted content and identifies relevant safety information, such as the possible threat type, credibility indicators, and potential level of concern.
**Step 4 — Receive Results**
The application displays a clear result using a Low, Medium, or High safety level and provides an understandable credibility assessment.
**Step 5 — Follow Safety Guidance**
SANAD provides recommendations and safety information relevant to the situation.
**Step 6 — Verify Important Information**
For serious situations, users are encouraged to confirm information through official emergency services and trusted authorities.


Target Users
SANAD is designed for a wide range of users who may encounter crisis-related information in their daily lives.
The application can be useful for:
Students who encounter emergency information through social media or messaging platforms.
Families and citizens who want to better understand potentially dangerous information.
Individuals who need quick access to basic safety guidance.
Communities that want to improve awareness and preparedness during emergencies.
SANAD is designed to be simple enough for users without technical or AI knowledge.



What Makes SANAD Different?
SANAD combines AI-based information analysis, crisis awareness, and safety education into one user-friendly application.
Instead of simply displaying emergency information, SANAD is designed to help users understand what the information may mean and what they should consider doing next.
The application brings together several functions in one platform:
Crisis-specific AI analysis
Safety-level classification
Information credibility assessment
Threat identification
Personalized safety recommendations
Emergency Mode
Safety education
Trusted-source verification
This combination aims to make complex crisis information easier for everyday users to understand.

Safety Disclaimer
SANAD is an educational and awareness project designed to demonstrate how artificial intelligence can support crisis awareness and information analysis.
SANAD does not replace emergency services, government authorities, medical professionals, law enforcement, or official emergency alerts.
AI-generated results may not always be accurate and should not be treated as confirmed facts. During a real emergency, users should always follow instructions from official authorities and trusted emergency services.
The application is intended to encourage awareness, preparedness, and responsible information verification.
