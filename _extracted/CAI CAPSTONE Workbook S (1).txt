G11 Term 3 – Project Overview 
 
The CAI Capstone Project gives you the opportunity to apply the skills and knowledge you have learned at ATS 
to solve a real-world problem using AI technologies. You will design, develop, test, and improve a digital or AI-
based solution while demonstrating creativity, problem-solving, and technical skills. 
Throughout the project, you will plan your work, document your progress, and collaborate with your team, similar 
to how professionals work in technology fields. The project encourages innovation and the use of modern AI 
tools and technologies to create a high-quality final product. 
Outstanding projects may be showcased at the school’s TVET event. This capstone project is your chance to 
demonstrate your readiness for future studies and careers in computing and AI while creating a project you can 
participate in multiple showcase competitions and innovation awards. 
 
G11 Term 3- Project Planning and Proposal Phase 
Term 3 Weekly Plan: Project Planning and Proposal 
 This term, you will start planning your Capstone Project, which you will fully implement and complete in 
Grade 12, Term 1. 
You will explore project ideas, investigate different technical approaches, and develop a clear concept that 
is both practical and innovative. You will work like a professional AI /Software engineer, considering the 
end-user and market need in your plan wh ile exploring the recent AI tools and technologies for optimal 
utilization in your project. This ensures your ideas are realistic, well -organized, and ready to be brought to 
life. 
By the end of week two, you will create a complete project proposal. This proposal will explain what you 
plan to build, why it is important, and how you will carry it out. It will be submitted to the ATS Directorate 
CDU Specialist for review and approval. Once approved, your proposal will guide all your planning and 
practical work in Grade 12, Term 1, helping you stay focused and on track to produce a high-quality project.

Week 1–2: Explore and Select Project Concept 
Form a project group of no more than four students, ensuring members demonstrate strong communication 
skills, teamwork, responsibility, time management, problem-solving ability, and commitment, as these soft 
skills are essential for successful project completion. 
• Brainstorm multiple project ideas with guidance from your supervisor teacher. 
• Conduct preliminary research to understand real-world applications and existing solutions. 
• Discuss ideas with peers and your supervisor to get feedback and advice. 
• Select one project idea to develop further. 
• Define the project’s goal, purpose, expected outcomes, and the problem it aims to solve, with 
supervisor input. 
Week 3–5: Prepare and Submit Project Proposal 
• Conduct research on software, tools, technologies, GenAI & Agentic AI principles relevant to your 
project. 
• Develop a detailed project proposal under the guidance of your supervisor, including: 
o Project title and description 
o Problem statement 
o Background research 
o Project scope 
o Platforms, AI tools, software and any resources required 
o Expected outcomes 
• Submit the completed proposal to the Computing CDU Specialist by the end of Week 4–5. 
• Ensure your supervisor reviews your proposal before submission for accuracy and completeness.

G12 Term 1 overview   
 
Welcome to Term 1 of your CAI Capstone Project! 
This term focuses on turning your approved plan into a fully working computing or AI solution. You will 
develop, test, improve, and finalize your project while applying your programming, problem-solving, and AI 
skills. 
With support from your supervisor, you will manage your time, document your progress, solve technical 
challenges, and work effectively as a team. 
At the end of the term, you will present your project at the Capstone Showcase, demonstrating your solution and 
reflecting on what you learned. This is your opportunity to showcase your skills, creativity, and innovation. 
 
G12 Term 1- Project Implementation Phase 
 
Term 1 Weekly Plan: Project Production, Testing and Presentation 
 Week 1–2: Project Development Begins  
• Start developing your approved project under the guidance of your supervisor. 
• Set up your development environment and project structure. 
• Begin coding, building AI models, or integrating system components based on your plan. 
• Create system diagrams, flowcharts, UI designs, or architecture diagrams to guide 
development. 
• Conduct initial testing and debug early issues. 
Remember: 
Take screenshots, screen recordings, or photos of your progress and keep clear notes in your 
project log. These will serve as evidence of your work. 
Week 3–4: Project Development and Testing 
• Continue development, coding, and system integration under supervisor guidance. 
•  Test individual features and AI components to ensure functionality, accuracy, and 
efficiency. 
• Debug issues and refine your solution based on testing results.
