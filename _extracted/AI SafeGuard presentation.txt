===== PAGE 5 =====
Problem Statement
Workers in workshops, laboratories and industrial
environments must wear personal protective equipment
to reduce the risk of injury. However, some workers may
forget or ignore these requirements.
Traditional monitoring depends heavily on supervisors,
who may miss violations because of distractions, large
work areas or demanding workloads. Manual incident
recording can also be slow and inconsistent.
Supervisors therefore need a reliable system that can
check safety compliance in real time, identify workers and
maintain accurate incident records. AI SafeGuard
addresses this need through automated PPE detection,
facial recognition, immediate alerts and incident logging.

===== PAGE 6 =====
AI SafeGuard is an AI-powered monitoring system designed for
workshops, laboratories and industrial workplaces.
A webcam captures an image of a worker at an entry point. The system
uses a custom YOLO11s model to detect visible safety equipment and
DeepFace facial recognition to identify the worker. It then compares the
detected equipment with the requirements configured for that entry
point.
If the worker meets all requirements, the system displays a compliant
result. If equipment is missing, it produces an audible alert and records
the worker’s identity, entry point, missing equipment and scan image.
Supervisors can use the dashboard to review incidents, manage workers
and entry points, and monitor compliance information.

Resources and Materials Used
01
The active PPE model uses Ultralytics YOLO11s
with PyTorch. OpenCV processes camera
images, while YuNet detects faces and
Facenet512 generates facial embeddings for
worker identification. DeepFace supports the
facial-recognition workflow.
02
The frontend uses React, Vite, HTML,
CSS and JavaScript. The FastAPI
backend manages image uploads, AI
processing, worker records and
incident data, while Uvicorn runs the
application locally.
03
Version 1 used approximately 68
curated Roboflow datasets and was
trained in Kaggle with an NVIDIA Tesla
T4 GPU. Version 2 used locally recorded
camera videos with manually reviewed
annotations.
04
The required hardware consists
of a development laptop and an
external USB webcam.

Challenges and Solutions
RetinaFace took 9–
16 seconds to
detect a face, so
we replaced it with
YuNet. This
reduced face-
detection time to
approximately
0.05–0.10 seconds
while keeping
Facenet512 for
identity matching.
The public datasets
used different class
names and
annotation formats.
We cleaned and
combined
approximately 68
datasets, corrected
annotation
problems, and
standardized them
into six PPE classes.
Training the YOLO
model locally was
very slow and
caused the laptop
to overheat. We
moved the training
process to Kaggle
and used an
NVIDIA Tesla T4
GPU to complete it
reliably.
Version 2 missed
some equipment
under our webcam
conditions. We
recorded local videos,
manually corrected
the annotations, and
fine-tuned the model,
increasing frame-
level recall from 45%
to 100% on our
separate test video.

AI can produce

support safety

make final

Worker photographs

Facial recognition

results.
Poor lighting, hidden

===== PAGE 14 =====
AI Implementation
PPE Detection
The system captures an
image using the webcam
and sends it to the YOLO11s
model. The model analyzes
the image and detects six
types of PPE: helmets,
goggles, gloves, safety vests,
ear protection, and lab
coats.
Facial Recognition
YuNet locates the worker’s
face in the captured image.
Facenet512 then creates a
facial embedding and
compares it with the saved
embeddings of registered
workers to determine the
worker’s identity.
Compliance Decision
The system compares the
detected PPE with the safety
equipment required at the
selected entry point. It then
displays a compliant or violation
result, identifies any missing
equipment, and records the
incident when necessary.
The AI SafeGuard project integrates artificial
intelligence to improve workplace safety
through automatic PPE detection and worker
identification.

===== PAGE 18 =====
Conclusion
AI SafeGuard addresses the problem of missed PPE
violations by automatically detecting safety
equipment, identifying registered workers, and
recording incidents in real time. Our final model
achieved 95.9% recall and detected all six PPE classes
in our separate test video.
Throughout this project, we learned how to prepare
datasets, train AI models, develop a complete
application, and improve it through testing. 
The project shows how artificial intelligence can
support supervisors, improve safety compliance, and
help create safer workplaces.
