===== PAGE 3 =====
Idea Generation &
Evaluation
Initial Brainstorming Alternatives
We evaluated several standalone concepts during early
ideation: 1) A barcode-only scanner app, 2) A static
household budget tracker, and 3) A simple recipe
recommendation tool based on fridge contents.
Why We Chose Shop Wise AI Cart
Users face fragmented experiences using multiple apps
for budgets, diets, and allergies. Our evaluation proved
that uniting all these capabilities into an AI-powered
camera assistant offers unmatched convenience and
market value.

===== PAGE 5 =====
Project
Overview
All-in-One Solution: Combines camera recognition, budget control, allergy
alerts, store navigation, and expiry reminders into a single unified app.
Supermarket Integration: Select specific stores like Lulu Hypermarket or
Carrefour to match local inventory, aisle layouts, and pricing.
Personalized AI Guidance: Adapts to user profiles over time, learning shopping
habits and making tailored product suggestions.

===== PAGE 6 =====
Conducted Research &
Competitor Review
Market & Competitor Analysis
Existing supermarket apps offer basic online
ordering or simple loyalty points, but lack real-
time camera-based nutritional scanning, instant
budget threshold warnings, and integrated
allergy safety checks.
Key User Research Findings
Over 80% of surveyed shoppers reported
accidentally exceeding their planned grocery
budget and expressed anxiety over hidden
allergens in packaged goods. This validated our
core AI camera and smart budget features.
